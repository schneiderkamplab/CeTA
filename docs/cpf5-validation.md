# CPF 5 support and validation

The CPF 5 milestone is complete. Ferrum emits structured CPF 5 XML for ITS and
higher-order rewriting; unified CeTA checks it, including embedded first-order
delegation children. CPF 5 inherits CPF 3 and CPF 4. LeTA and CPF 6 implementation
remain deferred. This milestone does not claim completeness of proof search.

## Validation

All 22 acceptance gates passed on one immutable unified checker. The checks
include 596 CPF 3 compatibility cases, all 4,821 retained CPF 4 certificates
twice, all 2,674 retained CPF 5 mode/certificate entries twice, and 455 additional
higher-order source-bound checks. Regression coverage includes all 20 existing
higher-order root forms (287 cases, twice), 95 ITS version/claim cases, 24
inherited first-order cases, four explicit legacy cases and 45 ITS integration
tests. The original 170 unresolved first-order inputs are no-proof results,
not rejected certificates.

Fresh oracle-guided replay covers the same 1,371 inputs in each mode:

| Mode | ITS YES / NO | HO YES / NO | Certified | Unknown | Search timeout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strict | 625 / 489 | 203 / 25 | 1,342 | 29 | 0 |
| Learned | 622 / 486 | 202 / 25 | 1,335 | 30 | 6 |

All baseline successes were retained. Learned replay adds three certified YES
results. Final audits independently validate schema, source binding, polarity,
version, checker identity and every unresolved disposition. The six timeouts
are search timeouts; they are not certification failures. There are 2,677 fresh
certified mode entries and 65 unresolved mode entries, not 2,742 distinct inputs.
Concurrency differs from the historical run, so these are coverage results,
not a matched runtime comparison. The oracle and production guidance are unchanged.
